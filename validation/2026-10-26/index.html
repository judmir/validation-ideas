Full proposal from the brief

briefs/2026-10-26.md · “Validation page proposal (winner p-100; content only, NOT deployed)”

Headline (a complainer's own words):

"When there are changes to the games, why in the heck doesn’t the app tell me? I have to check it manually constantly."

Subhead: Every kid, every team app, one schedule. Paste each team's calendar link once and get a push the moment a game moves. Works on Android, iPhone and the web.

3 benefits

  1. All teams in one place. TeamSnap, GameChanger, SportsEngine, Heja and more, color-coded by kid, right in the calendar you already use.
  2. Know the second a game moves. A push alert when a game or practice is moved, added or cancelled, plus a flag when two kids need you in two places.
  3. Setup in a minute, no logins. Paste a link, or snap a screenshot of any schedule. Grandparents follow along free.

Price: $3.99/month (or $29.99/year) for the whole family, estimate.

Flow animation (4 steps)

  1. Three team-app icons (TeamSnap, GameChanger, Heja) with a "Copy calendar link" tap.
  2. Paste into KidSlate → "Maya · U10 Soccer" and "Leo · 12U Baseball" appear in two colors.
  3. One week view fills up; a red "overlap Sat 10:00" flag shows.
  4. A phone lock screen gets a push: "Leo's game moved to 11:30, Field 4."

Buttons

  • Main: "Start for $3.99/month" → fake checkout screen: "Thanks! We're not open yet. You'll be first in line, and we'll email you before anything is charged." (no payment taken)
  • Softer link: "Join waitlist" (email only)
  • Note under the buttons: "In development, join early access."

Suggested ad angle: a 10-second phone screen recording: three team apps → one color-coded week → a "game moved" push. Hook: "Two kids, three team apps, one missed game? Get every game in one schedule." Target US parents 30–50 with youth-sports interests (TeamSnap, GameChanger, Little League, AYSO, club soccer), Android and iPhone; run in-season (now: fall soccer/basketball start).
