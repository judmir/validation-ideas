All 13 pieces, from the builder's POC note (poc/2026-10-26.md)
  • RealAdd team by iCal/webcal link

    Real. Server-side fetch (webcal→https, 15 s timeout, no logins, no partner APIs), then node-ical (recurring events expanded; window from 3 weeks back to 6 months ahead). App auto-detected from the URL, PRODID or calendar name; kid guessed from the title.

  • RealReal public feeds

    Real, tested live tonight: Broadalbin-Perth HS (NY) on ScheduleGalaxy (501 events, 294 in window, ~300 ms), Clinton Public School (NJ) on Google Calendar (179/36, ~270 ms), Caledonia HS (MI) on EventLink (997/298, ~200 ms). Whole-school feeds get an "Only keep events containing…" keyword filter per team (e.g. "Soccer").

  • RealTeamSnap / GameChanger / SportsEngine feeds

    Realistic sample exports (no public feeds exist without a parent's own token), served from the app at /samples/*.ics with app-style PRODIDs, UIDs, TZIDs and calendar names. They go through the same node-ical path.

  • RealKid guessing

    Real heuristics: names you typed, else "Maya Rivera – …", "Leo's schedule", "events for Ava". If there's no name (school feeds), the app asks once: "Whose team is this?"

  • RealMerge + color per kid + week view

    Real (client, shared components/core.js). On Sundays it opens on the week ahead.

  • RealOverlap flag

    Real: timed, not-cancelled events of different kids whose times intersect. Shown as a day banner plus an orange ring and tag on each event.

  • RealChanges feed / change diff

    Real diff by UID between the stored snapshot and a fresh fetch: moved (time or place), added, cancelled (STATUS:CANCELLED, "Cancelled" in the title, or removed from the feed). Simulate refresh swaps in a second snapshot of the GameChanger sample (game moved 10:00 → 11:30 AM at Field 4, extra practice added, Wednesday practice cancelled). Refresh now re-fetches every link for real.

  • MockedPush alert

    Mock in-app banner labelled "mock push" (no real push, as briefed).

  • RealScreenshot import

    Real vision AI. The image is downscaled in the browser, then the server calls claude-haiku-4-5 and gets back JSON events. Tested on a rendered sportsYou-style schedule screenshot: 6/6 events with the right date and time, the cancelled practice flagged, and team and app recognised, in ~4.0 s.

  • Real"Add to my calendar"

    Real. A webcal://…/api/merged?s=… subscribable feed (the server re-fetches every team link on each calendar poll; the team list lives in the link, so there's no account or storage) plus a client-side Download merged .ics. Each event is prefixed with the kid's name, e.g. "Maya · Game vs. Hawks".

  • Fake (demo)Paywall + checkout

    Fake, marked "DEMO · NO REAL PAYMENTS". $3.99/month or $29.99/year for the whole family; 2 teams free, the 3rd team opens the paywall; the "Start for…" button only shows "Thanks! We're not open yet…". No card fields, no charge.

  • MockedGrandparent link

    Mock (makes up a link locally and shares nothing).

  • Not builtAccounts / RSVP / chat / carpool / two-way sync / native apps

    Not built (skipped as briefed).
