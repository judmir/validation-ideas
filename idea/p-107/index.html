Who has the pain

US parents of daycare children whose centers use Procare or brightwheel (App Store reviewers)

US daycare parents can't easily keep their child's daycare photos: Procare has no album view or bulk download, so each photo is opened and saved one by one, and brightwheel's update limited how far back photos can be seen.

All quotes (3)
  • “Photo viewing and invoice detail improvements needed Need to add the ability to view all photos in an album-like setting, also with the ability to select and bulk download photos all at once. Currently have to view and download each photo individually. Also the invoicing doesn’t show a breakdown of what is being paid for for a particular statement, for instance if paying for tuition, it should should the time period that tuition is being paid for. If there are additional items, such as late fees or late pickup fees, those should be itemized on the statement so you know exactly what you’re paying for.
