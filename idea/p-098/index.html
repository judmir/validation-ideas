Who has the pain

US K-12 parents whose district uses Edulog or Stopfinder (App Store/Play reviewers)

US parents can't rely on district school-bus tracking apps (Edulog Parent Portal, Stopfinder): alerts arrive after the bus has left, substitute buses aren't tracked, late buses vanish from the map, logins time out so delay alerts never arrive, and siblings trigger duplicate alerts.
