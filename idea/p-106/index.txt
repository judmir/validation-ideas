It’s mind boggling how you can push an app out that literally has so useful and essential features missing. There’s no way to sync workouts to the Apple Health app, which means the workouts don’t count towards Apple Watch goals at all. There’s no way to take workout data that’s input or synced into app and export that data into workout logs app (e.g. Strong). You can favorite other gyms (like if one gym is closer to home and another work) but how do you VIEW favorites since they don’t show up on the home screen? There’s no way to see what is showing in the cardio theater. There’s no way to get eGym analytics (outside of Bioage, but most of the other stuff can only be accessed from the main hub/console). So basically you have ZERO ACCESS to YOUR own data and they basically skim it down to the bare minimum and give you access to that. Whoever is in charge of the app you’re missing the boat and leaving EOS vulnerable - between the slow scale to rollout more locations and ease congestion and all the misses that every other gym already has figured out the value proposition for staying at EOS is quickly fading - especially seeing as none of these things are being addressed in any sort of timely manner.
